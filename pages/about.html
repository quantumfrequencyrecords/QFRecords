THE FREQUENCY

A DEAL THAT GETS BETTER AS YOU DO.

Quantum Frequency Records was built as the opposite of the traditional major-label model. We do not take perpetual ownership of your masters. We do not keep the same high percentage no matter how successful you become. We do not bury artists in unrecouped debt while the label profits from day one.

Instead, every partnership uses a progressive royalty structure: the label’s share starts higher while risk is greatest and steadily declines as the project earns more. Artists retain ownership of their masters from day one, with clear term limits and a transparent buyout path. Quality over quantity. Transparency over fine print. Partnership that rewards success instead of punishing it.

WHY QFR

YOUR SUCCESS SHOULD NOT COST YOU MORE.

Traditional deals often lock in a high label percentage for the life of the copyright and keep ownership of the masters forever. The more an artist earns, the more the label takes in absolute terms while the artist’s relative share stays low. We reject that structure completely.

PROGRESSIVE ROYALTIES. ARTIST-OWNED MASTERS. CLEAR TERM. TRANSPARENT BUYOUT.
HOW THE DEAL WORKS

A STRUCTURE THAT REWARDS GROWTH.

Most labels take the same high percentage no matter how big the project becomes. We do the opposite. Our progressive royalty scale starts higher when risk is greatest and declines as success grows — so the artist keeps more of the upside.

Artists retain ownership of their masters from day one. Every agreement has a defined term and a clear, calculable buyout. When the term ends or the buyout is exercised, the catalog belongs fully to the artist.

The label only wins when the music wins. That is the entire model.
