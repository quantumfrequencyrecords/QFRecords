THE FREQUENCY

SOUND SHOULD MOVE YOU.

Quantum Frequency Records exists for music that creates a reaction. Music that makes you stop, listen, think, feel and come back for another listen.

We are building a home for artists and sounds that live beyond traditional boundaries — from rock and country to electronic, industrial, alternative, psychedelic and everything in between.

WHY QFR

NO SINGLE SOUND DEFINES US.

QFR is intentionally genre-fluid. Our roster can move from country rock to nu-metal, progressive rock to electronic music, psychedelic rock to punk and beyond.

THE ONLY RULE IS THAT IT HAS TO SOUND LIKE SOMETHING WORTH HEARING.
THE NEXT FREQUENCY

WHERE TECHNOLOGY MEETS CREATIVITY.

Quantum Frequency Records embraces new creative technology as another instrument in the creative process.

Our catalog includes artists and music created through AI-assisted workflows, including music developed with generative tools such as Suno. These tools allow us to experiment with voices, arrangements, sounds and entire musical identities at a scale that would have been difficult to imagine only a few years ago.

But technology is not the destination. The music is.
