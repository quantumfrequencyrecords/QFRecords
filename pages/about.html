THE FREQUENCY

A DEAL THAT GETS BETTER AS YOU DO.

Quantum Frequency Records was built as the opposite of the traditional major-label model. We do not take perpetual ownership of your masters. We do not keep the same high percentage no matter how successful you become. We do not bury artists in unrecouped debt while the label profits from day one.

Instead, every partnership uses a progressive royalty structure: the label’s share starts higher while risk is greatest and steadily declines as the project earns more. Artists retain ownership of their masters from day one, with clear term limits and a transparent buyout path. Quality over quantity. Transparency over fine print. Partnership that rewards success instead of punishing it.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

DISCOVER. SUPPORT. ALIGN. RELEASE.

01

DISCOVER

We look for artists who refuse to make safe, disposable music. Unique voices, real ideas, and the courage to take risks. If it makes someone stop and turn the volume up, we want to hear it.

02

SUPPORT

We provide infrastructure, expertise, and creative space so artists can focus on the work. No artificial deadlines. No pressure to release unfinished or compromised material just to hit a calendar date.

03

ALIGN

Every deal uses a progressive royalty scale: higher label share on early revenue, lower share as success grows. Artists keep ownership of their masters. Contracts have defined terms and a clear, calculable buyout. Both sides win bigger when the music wins bigger.

04

RELEASE

We release music when it is ready. One record people return to for years is worth more than twenty average ones. We measure success by longevity and impact, not by how many tracks we can push out in a quarter.

WHY QFR

YOUR SUCCESS SHOULD NOT COST YOU MORE.

Traditional deals often lock in a high label percentage for the life of the copyright and keep ownership of the masters forever. The more an artist earns, the more the label takes in absolute terms while the artist’s relative share stays low. We reject that structure.
